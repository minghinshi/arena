# ARENA solutions

My solutions for the [Alignment Research Engineer Accelerator (ARENA)](https://www.arena.education/) [course materials](https://learn.arena.education/). ARENA is a technical AI safety programme where students are taught the basics of doing research that ensures the safety of AI agents/models.

ARENA is divided into five chapters. Each chapter consists of many parts. My solutions are located at `[chapter]/exercises/[part]/answers.py`.

Note: I have not participated ARENA in-person.
